## Notes

### Recommended Testing Environment

- **Browser:** Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge
- **Device:** Laptop or desktop computer
- **Mobile testing:** Use a mobile device or the browser’s responsive-device mode
- **Internet connection:** Required to access the GitHub Pages links

### Login and Role Selection

The prototype includes three demonstration roles:

- **Student**
- **Student Tutor**
- **Administrator**

Select the required role from the Role Selection page to access its workflow. The prototype uses demonstration accounts and sample data. If the prototype provides pre-filled login details, use those details to continue.

### Suggested Testing Flow

#### Student Workflow

1. Open the homepage.
2. Select **Find a Tutor**.
3. Search or filter the tutor list.
4. Open a tutor profile.
5. Submit a tutoring request.
6. Check the request status on the Student Dashboard.
7. Complete the simulated payment after tutor approval.
8. View payment history.
9. Leave a review and rating after the session is completed.

#### Student Tutor Workflow

1. Select the Student Tutor role.
2. Submit a tutor application.
3. Create or review the tutor profile after approval.
4. Open the Tutor Dashboard.
5. Review incoming tutoring requests.
6. Approve or reject a request.
7. View tutoring schedules, reviews, earnings, and payment status.

#### Administrator Workflow

1. Select the Administrator role.
2. Open the Admin Dashboard.
3. Review and approve or reject tutor applications.
4. Manage user accounts and platform records.
5. Verify simulated student payments.
6. Record platform commission and update tutor payouts.
7. Review dashboard statistics and platform activity.

### Demo Data

- All names, tutor profiles, tutoring requests, reviews, and payment records are sample or masked data.
- No real passwords, personal information, or financial information are used.
- Payment actions are simulations and do not process real transactions.
- The sample usability-testing dataset is synthetic coursework data and should not be treated as actual participant research.

### Prototype Limitations

- The prototype is intended to demonstrate the required workflows and interface design.
- Some records may use local storage, JSON, static sample data, or simulated storage.
- Authentication and user-role access may be simulated.
- Tutor approval, payment verification, commission recording, and earnings transfer do not perform real financial transactions.
- Email, notification, file-upload, and payment integrations may be represented through prototype interactions or confirmation messages.
- Refreshing the page or clearing browser data may reset locally stored demonstration records.
