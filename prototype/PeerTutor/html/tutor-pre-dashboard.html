Frequently Asked Questions

Before you apply

How long does tutor approval take?

For this MVP, the administrator reviews tutor applications manually. The expected review time is approximately one to two business days.

Can I edit my application after submitting it?

You can update the information before final submission. Changes after submission may require contacting the administrator.

Can I teach more than one subject?

Yes. You can select multiple subjects or courses that match your knowledge and academic experience.

Need help with your application?

Contact the PeerTutor administrator if you have questions about tutor eligibility, documents, or approval.
