Privacy, safety, and responsible platform use

Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

This page explains how PeerTutor handles user information and the responsibilities of students, student tutors, and administrators when using the platform.

Effective date: July 22, 2026 Last updated: July 22, 2026

Educational MVP Notice

PeerTutor is currently an educational prototype developed for ICT105 Fundamental Technology Entrepreneurship. Authentication, payment processing, payment verification, notifications, and some administrative functions are simulated for prototype demonstration and usability testing.

Users should not enter real passwords, banking credentials, identification documents, payment-card numbers, or other highly sensitive personal information into the prototype.

Privacy Overview

PeerTutor is a student-to-student tutoring marketplace designed to help university students find, compare, and request tutoring sessions from verified student tutors.

PeerTutor follows privacy-by-design and data minimization principles. This means the platform should request only the information reasonably necessary to provide tutoring-related services, maintain platform safety, and support administrative management.

Minimum Data

Collect only information required for tutoring activities.

Transparency

Explain what data is collected and why it is needed.

Controlled Access

Limit access according to the Student, Tutor, and Administrator roles.

2

Information We Collect

The information collected depends on the user role and the features used.

User group Possible information Purpose
Students Name, university email, selected subjects, tutoring requests, session details, and reviews. To find tutors, request sessions, track request status, and submit feedback.
Student Tutors Name, university email, subjects, qualifications, availability, hourly rate, profile information, and application status. To review tutor applications, create tutor profiles, and manage tutoring requests.
Administrators Administrative account details, approval records, verification decisions, and management activities. To approve tutors, manage users, verify simulated payments, and monitor the prototype.
All users Basic technical information such as browser type, device type, and locally stored preferences. To improve usability, compatibility, and platform performance.

Do not submit national identification numbers, passport information, real bank-account passwords, payment-card security codes, or unrelated sensitive personal information.

3

How Information Is Used

PeerTutor may use submitted information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing Student, Student Tutor, and Administrator accounts.
  • Displaying tutor subjects, qualifications, prices, ratings, and availability.
  • Processing and tracking tutoring requests.
  • Allowing tutors to accept, reject, or manage tutoring requests.
  • Allowing administrators to review tutor applications.
  • Simulating payment submission, verification, earnings, and payout records.
  • Collecting reviews, ratings, and prototype usability feedback.
  • Detecting misuse and maintaining platform safety.
  • Improving the usability and functionality of the PeerTutor prototype.
4

Data Minimization

PeerTutor should collect only the minimum amount of information necessary for each function. Optional information should be clearly identified, and users should not be required to provide unrelated personal information.

Prototype test data should use fictional or anonymized information wherever possible. Test participants should be identified using participant codes instead of unnecessary personal identifiers.

Recommended test information

  • Participant code such as TU-01
  • General role such as Student or Tutor
  • Fictional tutoring-request details
  • Simulated payment reference numbers
  • Anonymous usability ratings
5

Information Sharing

PeerTutor does not sell personal information. Information should be shared only when required to provide platform functions.

  • Students may view public tutor-profile information.
  • Tutors may view information necessary to respond to tutoring requests.
  • Administrators may access information required for tutor approval, user management, and simulated payment verification.
  • Aggregated or anonymous results may be used in course reports, prototype evaluations, and presentations.

Personal contact details should not be made publicly visible unless the user has clearly agreed and the information is necessary for the tutoring activity.

6

Data Storage and Security

The current PeerTutor MVP may use browser local storage, JSON data, or Google Sheets for simulated prototype records. These technologies are used for demonstration and are not presented as a production-level security architecture.

A future production version should implement:

  • Secure authentication and password hashing.
  • Encrypted network communication.
  • Secure server-side data storage.
  • Role-based access control.
  • Audit logs for important administrator actions.
  • Secure payment-provider integration.
  • Data backup and recovery procedures.
  • Regular security testing and updates.

Role-based access

Students, Student Tutors, and Administrators should have access only to information and functions required by their respective roles.

7

User Rights

Subject to applicable requirements and prototype limitations, users may request to:

  • View the information associated with them.
  • Correct inaccurate profile information.
  • Delete unnecessary prototype information.
  • Withdraw from prototype testing.
  • Ask how their information is being used.
  • Report suspected misuse or unauthorized access.

Requests may be submitted through the PeerTutor Contact Us page or through the contact details provided at the end of this policy.

8

Cookies and Local Storage

The prototype may use browser local storage to remember demonstration data, selected roles, interface settings, or temporary session information.

Local storage remains on the user’s browser unless it is cleared. Users can remove this information through their browser settings. Clearing browser data may reset prototype records and preferences.

9

Age Requirements

PeerTutor is intended primarily for university students. Users should meet the minimum age required by their university and applicable local rules to use the service independently.

If the platform is later offered to younger users, additional consent, safeguarding, and verification requirements should be implemented.

10

Terms of Use

By using PeerTutor, users agree to use the platform responsibly, provide accurate information, respect other users, and comply with these terms.

The three prototype roles are:

Student

Searches for tutors, submits tutoring requests, tracks requests, and leaves appropriate reviews.

Student Tutor

Applies for approval, manages a tutor profile, and responds to tutoring requests.

Administrator

Reviews tutor applications, manages users, and monitors prototype activities.

11

Acceptable Use

Users must not:

  • Provide false qualifications or impersonate another person.
  • Harass, threaten, discriminate against, or abuse another user.
  • Upload malicious files, code, or misleading payment evidence.
  • Attempt to access another user’s account or restricted information.
  • Use the platform for fraud or unauthorized commercial activity.
  • Manipulate reviews, ratings, tutor approvals, or payment records.
  • Share another person’s private information without permission.
  • Use tutoring sessions to support academic dishonesty.

Administrators may restrict or suspend access when misuse is identified.

12

Payments and Refunds

Payment processing, student-payment verification, tutor earnings, and tutor payouts are simulated in the current MVP.

The prototype must not be treated as a real payment service. No real transfer is guaranteed by a payment status displayed in the prototype.

A future implementation should use an authorized payment provider and clearly define payment, cancellation, dispute, commission, and refund procedures before accepting real money.

13

Student Tutor Responsibilities

Student Tutors are expected to:

  • Provide accurate qualification information.
  • List subjects they are reasonably able to teach.
  • Maintain accurate availability and pricing.
  • Communicate respectfully with students.
  • Protect information received through tutoring requests.
  • Avoid completing assessed work on behalf of students.
  • Notify students when a session cannot be completed.

Tutor approval in the prototype demonstrates the intended workflow and does not represent professional licensing or certification.

14

Disclaimer and Limitation

PeerTutor is provided as an educational MVP. Prototype information, availability, ratings, payment records, notifications, and account functions may be simulated or incomplete.

PeerTutor does not guarantee specific academic results. Students remain responsible for their own coursework, decisions, and compliance with university academic-integrity policies.

15

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use may be updated when PeerTutor features, requirements, technologies, or legal responsibilities change.

The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page should be revised whenever material changes are made.
