# Demo Script

## Demo Objective

The objective of this demonstration is to show how PeerTutor provides a centralized student-to-student tutoring marketplace for Rangsit University students.

The demo must prove that:
- Students can search for and compare approved tutors.
- Students can view tutor profiles and submit tutoring requests.
- Tutors can register, submit applications, and manage requests.
- Students and tutors can track request and payment statuses.
- Students can make payments after tutor approval.
- Administrators can approve tutors, verify payments, and manage platform records.
- Users receive validation and confirmation messages.
- Each user role has an appropriate dashboard.
- The platform has a consistent, responsive, and privacy-conscious design.
- Major screens are connected to the project requirements and user stories.

## Detailed Presentation Script

### Step 1: Project Introduction

**Presenter: Thin Thiri Naing**

“Good morning/afternoon. We are TechTitans, and our project is called PeerTutor. PeerTutor is a student-to-student tutoring marketplace designed for Rangsit University students. Currently, students usually search for tutors through Facebook groups, LINE chats, friends, or senior students. Tutor information is scattered across different channels, making it difficult to compare qualifications, availability, prices, and reviews. PeerTutor solves this problem by bringing tutor discovery, tutoring requests, payments, reviews, and progress tracking together on one platform.”

### Step 2: Homepage and User Pathway

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“This is the PeerTutor homepage. It clearly displays the platform name, target users, problem, and main solution. Users can select ‘Find a Tutor’ to browse available tutors or ‘Get Started Now’ to begin registration. PeerTutor supports Student, Student Tutor, and Administrator roles. Each role has a different pathway and access to appropriate functions.”

### Step 3: Tutor Registration and Application

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“A student who wants to become a tutor must first complete the tutor-registration form. The applicant provides educational background, teaching experience, skills, and supporting information. The system checks required information and displays a warning if the application is incomplete or duplicated. After submission, the user receives a confirmation message. The application is then stored for administrator review.”

### Step 4: Tutor Search and Listing

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“This page displays all approved tutors. Students can search for a tutor by name or subject. They can also filter tutors by major, price, and rating. This allows students to find suitable tutors more efficiently than searching through several social media channels.”

### Step 5: Tutor Profile

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“After selecting a tutor, the student can view the complete tutor profile. The profile contains subjects, educational background, teaching experience, price, availability, ratings, and reviews. This information helps students compare tutors and make an informed decision.”

### Step 6: Tutoring Request

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“The student can now submit a tutoring request. The form collects the subject, preferred date and time, and an additional note. Required-field validation prevents incomplete information from being submitted. After successful submission, the system displays ‘Request submitted successfully,’ and the request is stored for the tutor to review.”

### Step 7: Request Approval and Tracking

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“The tutor can view incoming tutoring requests through the Tutor Dashboard. The tutor can approve or reject each request. The student can monitor the request through the Student Dashboard. Possible statuses include Pending, Approved, Rejected, Completed, and Cancelled. This keeps both users informed throughout the process.”

### Step 8: Online Payment

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“After the tutor approves the request, the student can access the payment page. The page displays the tutoring-session details, payment amount, and payment method. After the student submits the payment, the system displays ‘Payment completed successfully.’ The payment status is then updated and sent to the administrator for verification.”

### Step 9: Administrator Functions

**Presenter: Yan Naing Kyaw**

“The administrator manages important platform activities through the Admin Dashboard. The administrator can approve or reject tutor applications, manage accounts, verify student payments, record platform commission, transfer tutor earnings, delete invalid submissions, and update payment statuses. After verifying a payment, the system displays ‘Payment verified successfully,’ and the tutoring session can be confirmed.”

### Step 10: Dashboards and Payment History

**Presenter: Yan Naing Kyaw**

“The Student Dashboard displays request statuses, upcoming sessions, past sessions, and payment history. The Tutor Dashboard displays incoming requests, tutoring schedules, reviews, earnings, and payment statuses. The Admin Dashboard displays tutor applications, accounts, transactions, pending tutor payouts, platform commission, statistics, and charts.”

### Step 11: Review and Rating

**Presenter: Thae Su Win Thu**

“After completing a tutoring session, the student can leave a review and rating. The review appears in the tutor’s profile and Tutor Dashboard. This helps tutors understand their performance and helps other students make informed decisions.”

### Step 12: User-Testing Results

**Presenter: Shinn Thant Maung**

“We tested the PeerTutor prototype with 10 users: five students, three student tutors, and two administrators. They completed tasks based on their roles, including searching for tutors, submitting requests, managing applications, checking statuses, and reviewing payment information. We recorded task completion, user ratings, errors, comments, and suggested improvements. The results helped us identify the changes required before Lab 14.”

### Step 13: Responsive Design, Privacy and Traceability

**Presenter: Yan Naing Kyaw**

“PeerTutor uses a consistent layout, navigation system, colour scheme, typography, buttons, and dashboard design. The key pages and forms are usable on laptop and mobile screens. We also use sample or masked data during the demonstration and collect only the information necessary for the platform. Each major screen is connected to our functional requirements, user stories, and MVP feature list.”

## Closing Statement

**Presenter: Thin Thiri Naing**

“To conclude, PeerTutor provides a centralized and convenient way for Rangsit University students to find academic support from other students. Students can search for tutors, compare tutor information, submit requests, make payments, monitor progress, and leave reviews. Student tutors can manage their profiles, tutoring requests, schedules, reviews, earnings, and payment statuses. Administrators can maintain platform reliability by managing applications, accounts, payments, and records. PeerTutor reduces the time, confusion, and uncertainty involved in finding tutors through scattered communication channels. Our next step is to apply the user-testing feedback, complete the prioritized improvements, and prepare PeerTutor for the final Lab 14 demonstration. Thank you.”
