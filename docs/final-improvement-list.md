## 4. Priority Definition

- **Critical:** Must be fixed before the final presentation because it blocks a main workflow.
- **Important:** Should be fixed to improve the final prototype and reduce significant confusion.
- **Useful:** Improves quality and usability but does not block the final demonstration.
- **Future:** Suitable for development beyond the current course requirements.

## 5. Final Decision

The PeerTutor prototype **needs minor revision before Lab 14**.

No Critical issue was identified in the synthetic sample results. However, the Important issues involving payment visibility, supporting-document instructions, tutor-profile publishing, and payment-stage separation should be corrected and retested before the final demonstration
