# Landing Page Content

## Project Title

**PeerTutor – Student-to-Student Tutoring Marketplace**

## Target User
- Rangsit University students who need academic tutoring support (primary)
- Student tutors who want to promote their tutoring services (primary)
- Platform administrators are a secondary audience.

The initial MVP focuses on students at Rangsit International College (RIC).

## Headline

**Learn from Top Students at Your University**

## Subheadline

Connect with peer tutors who have successfully completed the classes you are taking. PeerTutor provides affordable, flexible, and effective academic support from fellow university students.

## Problem Statement

Students often search for tutors through Facebook groups, LINE messages, friends, and senior students. Information is distributed across different channels, making it difficult to find and compare suitable tutors.

Students may not easily know:
- Which subjects a tutor can teach
- Whether the tutor has relevant academic experience
- When the tutor is available
- How much a tutoring session costs
- Whether previous students were satisfied
- How to request and track a tutoring session

The earlier PeerTutor labs identified the need for a centralized tutoring platform. The Lab 08 validation activities also examined whether users could understand the platform, search for tutors, compare tutor profiles, submit tutoring requests, and view their request status.

## Solution Description

PeerTutor is a web-based student-to-student tutoring marketplace that helps university students find suitable peer tutors in one centralized platform.

Through the landing page, users can:

- Understand how PeerTutor works
- Browse top-rated student tutors
- Compare subjects, ratings, reviews, and hourly rates
- Find tutors according to their university major
- View detailed tutor profiles
- Start a tutoring-session request
- Apply to become a student tutor
- Contact PeerTutor support
- Subscribe to receive tutoring-session updates

The landing page directs students and student tutors to the appropriate parts of the PeerTutor MVP prototype.

## How PeerTutor Works

PeerTutor introduces the tutoring process in three simple steps:

1. **Find Your Tutor**  
   Browse student tutors by subject, university major, rating, price, and availability.

2. **Book a Session**  
   Select a suitable tutor and submit a tutoring-session request for an appropriate date and time.

3. **Ace Your Class**  
   Learn from students who have experience in similar subjects and use their academic support to improve your understanding.

## Benefits

### Affordable Rates

Students can find peer tutoring at student-friendly prices compared with many professional tutoring services.

### Peer Connection

Students can learn from other students who have recently completed or performed well in similar courses.

### Flexible Scheduling

Students can find tutors and request sessions that match their academic needs and available schedules.

### Verified Student Tutors

The MVP includes an administrator-review process for tutor applications before a tutor profile is approved.

### Easier Tutor Comparison

Users can compare tutors according to their subjects, ratings, reviews, majors, and hourly rates.

### Centralized Academic Support

PeerTutor brings tutor discovery, tutor profiles, session requests, user communication, and request tracking together in one platform.

## Responsible Data Message

PeerTutor follows privacy-by-design and data-minimization principles. The platform should collect only the information required for registration, tutor applications, tutoring requests, communication, prototype evaluation, and service updates.

The landing page includes a subscription field and live-support interface. Information submitted through these features should be used only for their stated purposes.

### Subscription Data

An email address submitted through the subscription section should only be used to provide PeerTutor service or tutoring-session updates.

### Live-Support Data

Users should not submit passwords, banking credentials, payment information, government identification numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the live-support chat.

### Prototype Data

Information collected during MVP testing may be used to:

- Demonstrate prototype functionality
- Evaluate landing-page usability
- Measure user interest and CTA engagement
- Collect user feedback
- Identify opportunities for improvement
- Support academic project documentation

Authentication, payment processing, payment verification, data storage, acquisition tracking, and selected dashboard functions are simulated for the MVP.

The tutor numbers, tutoring-session numbers, ratings, reviews, prices, savings percentages, and user statements displayed on the landing page are illustrative prototype content and should not be interpreted as verified operational results.
