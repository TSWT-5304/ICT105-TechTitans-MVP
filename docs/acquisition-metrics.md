# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose
Define how TechTitans will measure early user interest and landing page performance for PeerTutor across the selected go-to-market channels: Class Chat, Campus QR Poster, Instagram/Facebook, and RIC Student Club.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Number of students who opened the PeerTutor landing page | Count all landing page visits | Landing page analytics or spreadsheet | At least **40** views |
| M-02 | CTA Clicks | Number of students who clicked **Find a Tutor** or **Become a Tutor** | CTA Clicks ÷ Landing Page Views × 100 | Landing page analytics | At least **25% click-through rate** |
| M-03 | Demo Attempts | Number of users who opened the PeerTutor prototype | Count prototype visits | Prototype activity log | At least **15** demo attempts |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Number of users who submitted feedback after using the prototype | Count submitted feedback forms | Google Form or spreadsheet | At least **8** responses |
| M-05 | Interest Conversion | Percentage of visitors who continued from the landing page to the prototype | Demo Attempts ÷ Landing Page Views × 100 | Spreadsheet calculation | At least **20% conversion rate** |

## Sample Results

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Landing Page Views | **118** |
| CTA Clicks | **35** |
| CTA Click-through Rate | **29.7%** |
| Demo Attempts | **20** |
| Feedback Responses | **11** |
| Tutor Sign-ups/Testers | **6** |
| Interest Conversion | **16.9%** |

## Interpretation
- The PeerTutor landing page received **118 views**, which exceeded the target of **40** views during the pilot campaign.
- The **CTA click-through rate reached 29.7% (35 out of 118 views)**, exceeding the target of **25%**, indicating that the landing page content and call-to-action successfully attracted student interest.
- The prototype recorded **20 demo attempts**, exceeding the target of **15**, showing that many visitors continued to explore the PeerTutor prototype.
- **11 feedback responses** were collected, surpassing the target of **8**, providing useful suggestions for improving usability and functionality.
- The **Interest Conversion Rate reached 16.9%**, which is **below** the target of **20%**. This suggests that while many users clicked the CTA, fewer continued to actively explore the prototype.
- Among the promotional channels, **Instagram/Facebook** generated the highest number of landing page views, while **Class Chat** produced strong engagement through repeated sharing. **Campus QR Posters** generated fewer interactions and may require improved placement or additional promotion.
- The team should focus on simplifying the journey from the landing page to the prototype, improving navigation, and strengthening follow-up prompts to increase conversion in future iterations.
