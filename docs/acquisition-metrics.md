# Acquisition Metrics

## Purpose
Define how TechTitans will measure early user interest and landing page performance for PeerTutor across the selected go-to-market channels: Class Chat, Campus QR Poster, Instagram/Facebook, and RIC Student Club.

## Required Metrics

| Metric ID | Metric Name | What It Measures | Formula / Counting Method | Data Source | Target / Success Criterion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| M-01 | Landing Page Views | Number of students who opened the PeerTutor landing page | Count all landing page visits | Landing page analytics or spreadsheet | At least **40** views |
| M-02 | CTA Clicks | Number of students who clicked **Find a Tutor** or **Become a Tutor** | CTA Clicks ÷ Landing Page Views × 100 | Landing page analytics | At least **25% click-through rate** |
| M-03 | Demo Attempts | Number of users who opened the PeerTutor prototype | Count prototype visits | Prototype activity log | At least **15** demo attempts |
| M-04 | Feedback Responses | Number of users who submitted feedback after using the prototype | Count submitted feedback forms | Google Form or spreadsheet | At least **8** responses |
| M-05 | Interest Conversion | Percentage of visitors who continued from the landing page to the prototype | Demo Attempts ÷ Landing Page Views × 100 | Spreadsheet calculation | At least **20% conversion rate** |

## Interpretation
What will the group do if the metrics are weak?

Examples:
- Improve headline
- Change CTA text
- Simplify prototype flow
- Use a more relevant user channel
- Clarify benefit statement
