Common Questions
How do I book a tutoring session?

Browse tutors, click "View Profile", then "Book a Session" and choose a date and time that works for you.

How long does tutor approval take?

Tutor applications are reviewed within 3–5 business days. You'll receive an email with the decision.

Can I cancel or reschedule a session?

Yes — up to 24 hours before the session. Go to My Dashboard and click Reschedule or Cancel.

Is my payment information secure?

Absolutely. All payments are processed through Stripe with bank-level encryption.
