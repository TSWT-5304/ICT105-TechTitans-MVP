## Slide 2: Problem and Target Users

### Problem

Rangsit University students often search for tutors through scattered channels such as:

- Facebook groups
- LINE chats
- Friends and classmates
- Seniors and personal contacts

This makes it difficult to:

- Compare tutor qualifications and experience.
- Check subjects, availability, ratings, and prices.
- Confirm whether tutors are trustworthy.
- Manage tutoring requests and session statuses.
- Track payments and transaction information.

### Target Users

- **Students:** Rangsit University students who need academic support and want to find, compare, and request suitable tutors.
- **Student Tutors:** Qualified students who want to provide tutoring, manage requests, and track schedules, reviews, and earnings.
- **Administrators:** Platform managers who approve tutor applications, manage accounts, verify payments, and monitor platform activities.

---

## Slide 3: Evidence from Customer Discovery and Validation

### Customer-Discovery Findings

- Tutor information is scattered across different communication channels.
- Students need clearer tutor qualifications, availability, ratings, and prices.

## Slide 4: Solution and Value Proposition

### Solution

PeerTutor provides:

- Approved tutor listings
- Tutor search and filtering
- Detailed tutor profiles
- Structured tutoring requests
- Request-status tracking
- Simulated online payments
- Payment-history records
- Reviews and ratings
- Role-based dashboards
- Tutor approval and user management
- Payment verification and tutor-earnings management

### Value Proposition

> For Rangsit University students who experience scattered and unreliable tutor discovery, PeerTutor helps them find and manage suitable Student Tutors through searchable profiles, structured tutoring requests, status tracking, payment management, and role-based dashboards.

## Slide 5: Final Prototype Demonstration Flow

### Student Flow

1. Open the PeerTutor homepage.
2. Select the Student role.
3. Search and filter tutors.
4. Open a tutor profile.
5. Submit a tutoring request.
6. Receive submission confirmation.
7. Track the request status.
8. Complete the simulated payment.
9. View payment history.
10. Leave a review and rating.

### Student Tutor Flow

1. Register and submit a tutor application.
2. Receive Administrator approval.
3. Create a tutor profile.
4. Review incoming requests.
5. Approve or reject requests.
6. View schedules, reviews, earnings, and payment statuses.

### Administrator Flow

1. Review tutor applications.
2. Approve or reject applicants.
3. Manage user accounts.
4. Verify simulated payments.
5. Record platform commission.
6. Update tutor payouts.
7. Review dashboard analytics.

## Slide 8: Technical Architecture and Data Handling

### Technical Architecture

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- **Hosting:** GitHub and GitHub Pages
- **Prototype Storage:** Local Storage, JSON, JavaScript objects, and sample records
- **Analytics:** Power BI and documented product metrics
- **Testing Data:** CSV, Excel, Google Forms, or Google Sheets

### Main Data Entities

- Users
- Tutor Applications
- Tutor Profiles
- Tutoring Requests
- Payment Records
- Reviews and Ratings

### Responsible Data Handling

- Only necessary information is collected.
- Sample or masked data is used in the demonstration.
- No real passwords or sensitive financial information are displayed.
- Payment actions are simulations.
- Personal information is shown only where appropriate.
- The prototype follows privacy and data-minimization principles.
